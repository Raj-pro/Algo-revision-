Job Scheduling with Deadlines

A greedy strategy to choose profitable jobs that finish before their deadlines.

1. Schedule the jobs

Assumption: each job takes one time slot. Sort by profit from highest to lowest, then place each job in the latest free slot at or before its deadline.

2. Jobs sorted by profit

The simulator processes the most profitable jobs first.

Key idea: put a selected job as late as possible before its deadline. This keeps earlier slots available for jobs with tighter deadlines.
Example: deadline 3 means a job can occupy slot 1, 2, or 3.

3. Complexity

Simple implementation: O(nD)

n = number of jobs; D = maximum deadline. More advanced data structures can improve scheduling efficiency.

The classic greedy job sequencing problem assumes every job takes one unit of time and each job earns its profit only if completed by its deadline.
