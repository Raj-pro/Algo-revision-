4. Remember

  • BFS: queue, breadth first, shortest path in unweighted graphs.
  • DFS: stack or recursion, depth first, useful for cycle detection, components, and topological-sort methods.
  • With adjacency lists, both take O(V + E) time and O(V) auxiliary space.
