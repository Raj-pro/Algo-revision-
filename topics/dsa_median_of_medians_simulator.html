Median of Medians

A deterministic selection algorithm for finding the k-th smallest element in worst-case linear time.

2. Algorithm steps

Why groups of five?

Grouping into fives ensures the chosen pivot is sufficiently central: a constant fraction of the values are guaranteed to be no smaller and no larger than the pivot. This keeps the worst-case recurrence linear.

Worst-case time: Θ(n)

3. The idea, simply

1. Group: split the values into groups of at most five.
2. Find medians: sort each small group and take its middle value.
3. Pivot: recursively find the median of those medians.
4. Partition: split values into less than, equal to, and greater than the pivot.
5. Continue: recurse only into the partition containing the requested k-th item.
