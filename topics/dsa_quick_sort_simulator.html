2. What is happening?

Quick Sort does not merge sorted halves. It partitions around a pivot.

Three steps

1. Choose pivot: pick a value from the current section.
2. Partition: place values smaller than or equal to the pivot on its left, and larger values on its right.
3. Recurse: sort the left and right sections.
Average: Θ(n log n)

3. Complexity

Best / average

Partitions are reasonably balanced, giving logarithmically many levels and linear work per level.

Θ(n log n)

Worst case

Highly unbalanced partitions (for example, repeatedly splitting off one item) can lead to quadratic time.

Θ(n²)

Typical in-place Quick Sort uses about O(log n) average recursion-stack space; worst-case stack depth can be O(n), depending on implementation.
